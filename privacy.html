Privacy Policy

Salam Motion Bot · last updated 28 November 2026

Salam Motion Bot is a private publishing tool. It is used only by its owner to post the owner's own videos and images to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram, Threads, TikTok) on a schedule. It is not offered to other users.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read the channel's name and its list of recent uploads (to avoid posting the same video twice).
  • Instagram and Threads: permission to publish posts to the owner's accounts and to read their recent posts for the same reason.
  • TikTok: permission to read the account's display name and to upload videos as drafts to the owner's TikTok inbox (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload). The owner reviews and posts each draft in the TikTok app.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not access, collect or store data about other people.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access keys are stored only as encrypted secrets of the owner's GitHub repository and are used only to publish scheduled content. Published content is public on the respective platforms by design.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) and in the account settings of Instagram, Threads and TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services).
